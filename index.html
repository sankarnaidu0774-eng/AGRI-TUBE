FIELD NOTE № 042

Bring your soil back to life

Healthy soil is a living ecosystem. Start small: add mature compost, protect the surface with leaf mulch, rotate plant families each season, grow legumes to fix nitrogen, and avoid walking on wet beds.

The goal isn’t just to feed the plant—it’s to feed the soil that feeds it.
